## Card Image Dimensions
- Portrait cards (enemy, location, treachery): 300x419 (~0.716 aspect ratio)
- Landscape cards (act, agenda): 419x300 (~1.397 aspect ratio)

### Thumbnail crop positions
- Enemy: bottom half (art at bottom of portrait card)
- Location: top half (art at top of portrait card)
- Treachery: top half (art at top of portrait card)
- Agenda: left half (art on left of landscape card)
- Act: right half (art on right of landscape card)
